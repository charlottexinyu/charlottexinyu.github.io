作者信息

Jiangning He（School of Information Management and Engineering, Shanghai University of Finance and Economics, Shanghai 200433, China; and Key Laboratory of Interdisciplinary Research of Computation and Economics, Shanghai University of Finance and Economics, Shanghai 200433, China） · Weikun Wu（School of Information Management and Engineering, Shanghai University of Finance and Economics, Shanghai 200433, China） · Jiayi Guo（School of Information Management and Engineering, Shanghai University of Finance and Economics, Shanghai 200433, China; and Key Laboratory of Interdisciplinary Research of Computation and Economics, Shanghai University of Finance and Economics, Shanghai 200433, China） · Xiao Fang（Lerner College of Business and Economics and Institute for Financial Services Analytics, University of Delaware, Newark, Delaware 19716）

作者摘要要点

  • 文章用动态需求饱和模型解释逛商场时某类消费兴趣如何随时间衰减，并用循环神经网络表示购物意图。
  • 在真实线下购物数据上，模型优于比较方法，作者另用用户研究考察需求饱和在购物体验中的作用。
