My Advisor, Her AI, and Me: Evidence from a Field Experiment on Human–AI Collaboration and Investment Decisions

作者信息

Cathy (Liu) Yang（Department of Information Systems and Operations Management, HEC Paris, 78350 Jouy-en-Josas, France） · Kevin Bauer（Department of Business Informatics and Information Economics, Goethe University Frankfurt, 60323 Frankfurt am Main, Germany） · Xitong Li（Department of Information Systems and Operations Management, HEC Paris, 78350 Jouy-en-Josas, France） · Oliver Hinz（Department of Business Informatics and Information Economics, Goethe University Frankfurt, 60323 Frankfurt am Main, Germany）

作者摘要要点

  • 研究在欧洲储蓄银行开展实地实验，比较纯AI建议与由银行顾问保留最终决定权的人机投资建议。
  • 两类建议的质量相当，客户对人机建议的采纳率更高，在风险投资中尤为明显。后续实验把差异主要归于人类参与带来的情感吸引力。
