Cenying Yang（Department of Information Systems, College of Business, City University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong SAR, 999077, China） · Yihao Feng（Department of Computer Science, College of Natural Sciences, University of Texas at Austin, Austin, Texas, 78712, USA） · Andrew Whinston（Department of Information Systems, Risks, and Operations Management, McCombs Business School of Business, University of Texas at Austin, Austin, Texas, 78712, USA）

作者摘要要点

  • 作者用深度强化学习模拟生鲜零售商根据食品品质定价、并选择是否向消费者公开品质的策略。
  • 在模型的部分情形中，按品质定价可带来更强需求及更少浪费。当许多顾客低估品质时，公开品质信息还会提高利润。
