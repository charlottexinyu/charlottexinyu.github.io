Faster Deliveries and Smarter Order Assignments for an On‐Demand Meal Delivery Platform

平台履约与配送

作者信息

Wenzheng Mao（Advanced Institute of Business, School of Economics and Management TongJi University Shanghai China） · Liu Ming（School of Management and Economics The Chinese University of Hong Kong Shenzhen China） · Ying Rong（Data‐Driven Management Decision Making Lab, Antai College of Economics and Management Shanghai Jiao Tong University Shanghai China） · Christopher S. Tang（UCLA Anderson School of Management University of California Los Angeles California USA） · Huan Zheng（Data‐Driven Management Decision Making Lab, Antai College of Economics and Management Shanghai JiaoTong University Shanghai China）

作者摘要要点

  • 作者分析一家中国外卖平台的交易信息，研究配送早晚与复购，并将骑手经验和本地知识用于识别。延迟会减少后续订单，提前送达的收益较小。
  • 在基于这些结果的模拟中，按未来订单量进行派单，比只追求送达速度带来更多后续订单。
