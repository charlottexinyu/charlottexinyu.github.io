An Interpretable Preference Learning Model Admitting Dynamic and Context-Dependent Preferences

消费者偏好学习与推荐

期刊与工作论文

INFORMS Journal on Computing · 正式在线 2026-06-09

会议经历

尚无已核实的会议记录。

作者信息

Zice Ru（Center for Intelligent Decision-making and Machine Learning, School of Management, Xi’an Jiaotong University, Xi’an 710049, China） · Jiapeng Liu（Center for Intelligent Decision-making and Machine Learning, School of Management, Xi’an Jiaotong University, Xi’an 710049, China） · Miłosz Kadziński（Institute of Computing Science, Poznan University of Technology, 60-965 Poznań, Poland） · Xiuwu Liao（Center for Intelligent Decision-making and Machine Learning, School of Management, Xi’an Jiaotong University, Xi’an 710049, China; and Collaborative Innovation Center of China Pilot Reform Exploration and Assessment - Hubei Sub-Center, Hubei University of Economics, Wuhan 430205, China） · Xinlong Li（Nanyang Business School, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore 639798）

作者摘要要点

  • 新模型将用户评论转为带倾向的商品属性，并用贝叶斯方法表示随情境与时间变化的购买偏好。
  • 在三组真实数据上，模型的预测表现优于所比较的七种算法，同时能给出与属性有关的推荐解释。
