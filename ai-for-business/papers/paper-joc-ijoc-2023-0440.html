作者信息

Xuan Wei（Antai College of Economics and Management, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai 200040, China） · Mingyue Zhang（School of Business and Management, Shanghai International Studies University, Shanghai 201620, China） · Qingpeng Zhang（Musketeers Foundation Institute of Data Science, The University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong, China; and Department of Pharmacology and Pharmacy, The University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong, China） · Zhi Li（Antai College of Economics and Management, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai 200040, China） · Daniel Dajun Zeng（Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100045, China; and School of Artificial Intelligence, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 101408, China）

作者摘要要点

  • 研究用人工标注与算法预测之间的互补程度动态调整权重，将分散、带噪声的标注汇总。
  • 在八个真实众包任务上，该方法优于比较办法，且对所用算法的类型、数量与参数设置较为稳健。
